Chapter 6

Divides the defects of a government into negative and positive.
Negative defects:
- Insufficient power to carry out duties.
	- Argues that balancing the power relations between the assembly and the administration is imperative.
- Insufficient development of capacities and social feelings of citizens. 
	- Argues for the inclusion of the whole public in government affairs. 
Positive defects:
- General ignorance and incapacity of the controlling body.
- Controlling body's interests not in conjunction with the general welfare of the community.
