Chapter 8 in Classic Readings in Sociology.

- People want information about a person in order to understand how to act around them.
- Expressions and impressions: former can be controlled by the one expressing, latter is based on the observer's experiences and expressions of person being observed.
- People may separate behaviours into manipulable/governable and ungovernable to try and discern which is "real", since they are aware that an individual may fake behaviour to give off a certain impression. 
- Creates an infinite information game that relies on both parties concealing, discovering, obtaining false revelations and rediscovering. 
- Initial definition of a relationship formed through the first impression. Creates a moral character of projections. 
- Interest in disruptions despite precaution taken to avoid them: people play pranks or joke around to simulate a disruption (that is not taken seriously so as to not actually disrupt anything).
