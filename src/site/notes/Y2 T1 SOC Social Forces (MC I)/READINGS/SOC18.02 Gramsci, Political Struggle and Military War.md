- Military wars end when one army is unable to fight and the victorious one occupies its territory. 
- Political struggles are not as simple, similar to colonialism because after the initial conquest, the victors need to continue politics in their newly occupied territory. 
- Three types of war faced by the colonised:
	- War of movement
	- War of position
	- Underground warfare
- War of manoeuvre: literal physical war using force.
	- Economic factors can be used as a weapon to throw the enemy into disarray and lose faith in their own side and quickly organize troops and ideology to achieve their objective.
- War of position: composed of the entire organization of nations, not just soldiers and artillery.
	- For advanced states, this is the main type of war, and the war of manoeuvre is used as a tactic rather than as the main method of war.
	- Superstructures in civil society become the trenches in modern warfare.
The Transition from the War of Manoeuvre to the War of Position in the Political Field as well
- Leads to reinforcement of hegemony. 
- A war of position is decisive definitively in politics.
