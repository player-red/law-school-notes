In The Foucault Effect: Studies in Governmentality (pp. 87-104)

- Treatises that were framed as "advice to the prince" slowly began turning into treatises on the art of government near the middle of the sixteenth century. 
- Problem of deciding how to be ruled and by whom, double movement of state centralization and religious dissidence.

- The Prince: singular and external existence that transcended his principality. 
	- External link = fragile link that can easily be under threat from both external and internal threats.
	- Machiavelli's work is a treaty explaining how the Prince can maintain this link and thus his power.
- Anti-Machiavellian literature wanted to replace the rule of the prince with the art of the government.

Guillaume de La Perriere: Miroir Politique
- Defines 'governor' and 'governing': any sort of figure with authority and power over a household, children, souls, provinces, religious orders, families, etc.
- The practices of government mean that there are multiple forms of government and one person can hold multiple positions in it.
Three types of government:
- Self-government: morality
- Governing a family: economy
- Ruling the state: politics
To connect the three types, continuity needs to be established in the upwards and downwards directions:
- Upwards continuity: self-government -> ruling the state (pedagogical formation)
- Downwards continuity: ruling the state -> self government (police)
- Central term of continuity: governing a family = economy

- Art of government: how to introduce the economy to the management of the state.
- GdlP: Government is the right disposition of things, arranged so as to lead to a convenient end.
	- Government governs things: no mention of territory. 
	- Things are instead people and their relations with other things (wealth, customs, habits, climate, irrigation, fertility, etc.).

- End of sovereignty is the exercise of sovereignty.
- Finality of government is in the things it manages, the instruments are tactics used to govern.
	- Can be distinguished from sovereignty from its objective: there is a list of finalities that a government aims to achieve.
- Art of government based on the principles of law, equity and humanity.
- Contract theory enables the founding contract to derive the principles of the art of government. - Population made possible the derestriction of the art of government:
	- Eliminating the family model: population appears to be irreducible to the family.
	- Recentering the economy.
	- Family becomes an instrument to collect information.
