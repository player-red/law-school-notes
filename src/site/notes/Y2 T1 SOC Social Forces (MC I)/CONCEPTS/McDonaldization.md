Coined by [[Y2 T1 SOC Social Forces (MC I)/PEOPLE/George Ritzer\|George Ritzer]].
- Process of a society adopting the characteristics of a fast food restaurant.
- Based on [[Y2 T1 SOC Social Forces (MC I)/PEOPLE/Max Weber\|Max Weber]]'s theory of [[Y2 T1 SOC Social Forces (MC I)/CONCEPTS/Rationalization\|Rationalization]].

- Primary components: efficiency, calculability, predictability, control. 
- For example, the McDonaldization of education: standard classroom setups, pedagogy, etc. (with no attention paid to the fact that each student learns differently) reduces the quality of education, does not promote inquiry or creativity, failing to teach individuals how to think critically.
