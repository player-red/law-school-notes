- Coined by [[Y2 T1 SOC Social Forces (MC I)/PEOPLE/Max Weber\|Max Weber]].

- It is the replacement of traditions, values, emotions, etc. as motivators of behaviour in society with concepts based on rationality and reason.
- Described as an "iron cage", since it has the effect of trapping people who live within it.
- Many argue that rationalization leads to dehumanization and is often conflated with progress.
