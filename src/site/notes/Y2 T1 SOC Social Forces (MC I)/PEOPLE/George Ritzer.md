American sociologist who worked on [[Y2 T1 SOC Social Forces (MC I)/CONCEPTS/McDonaldization\|McDonaldization]]. (1940-present)

- "Something vs Nothing":
	- 'Nothing' refers to "a social form that is generally centrally conceived, controlled and comparatively devoid of distinctive substantive content": any sort of fast food joint, chain store, etc.
	- 'Something' is a "locally conceived and controlled social form that is comparatively rich in distinctive substantive content": local restaurants, small businesses, etc.
- Globalization, grobalism and glocalism.
